Welcome to Carebridge: Your Accessible Healthcare Assistant

Snap a photo of any prescription or medical report. We read it, translate it into your language, and help you take the next step — safely and on time.

Get Started See How It Works

The Problem

Medical words were never written for everyone

For elderly patients, people who read with difficulty, and those who don't speak English, a simple prescription can feel like a locked door. Confusing labels lead to missed doses, missed appointments, and real harm. Carebridge opens that door.

How It Works

Three simple steps

  1. 1

    Snap a photo

    Point your camera at a prescription, report, or pill bottle. No typing, no forms.

  2. 2

    We read it for you

    Carebridge scans the text and explains it simply, in your own language — written or spoken aloud.

  3. 3

    Stay on track

    Get medicine reminders, appointment alerts, and directions to the nearest hospital, all in one place.

Built For You

Made for the people who need it most

Elderly patients

Large text, simple steps, and gentle reminders that respect your pace.

Low-literacy readers

Instructions explained in plain words and read aloud, not just printed.

Non-English speakers

Every label and instruction translated into your local language and dialect.
